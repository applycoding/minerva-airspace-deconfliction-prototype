# Licence texts

These files are copied verbatim from the installed distributions' licence
folders. Subdirectories retain their distribution names and nested notices.
They cover Qt/PySide6/shiboken6, shapely and GEOS, pyproj and PROJ, numpy and
its bundled notices, and the packaging tool's licence and exception.
Third-party permissions remain independent of the repository's LICENSE.
See NOTICE.md for versions, source locations and airspace data attribution.

The installed PySide6-Essentials and shiboken6 distributions have no
`dist-info/licenses` folders. LGPL-3.0.txt and its referenced GPL-3.0.txt
were therefore copied verbatim from /usr/share/common-licenses/LGPL-3 and
/usr/share/common-licenses/GPL-3 on the verification host. No missing upstream
package-specific notices are represented as having been supplied.
